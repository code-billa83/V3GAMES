Memory Match Game

A free card matching brain game. Find every pair in as few moves as you can.

Flip two cards to find a pair

How to play Memory Match

  1. Tap any card to flip it over.
  2. Flip a second card to try to find its pair.
  3. Match all eight pairs in as few moves as possible.

Train your memory

Memory games help you practise recall and focus. Try to remember where each symbol is after it appears, and plan your next flip based on what you already saw.

Best strategy

Do not rush. Each card you reveal gives you information, so keep track of positions. Your best move count is saved on your device so you can keep improving.

Frequently asked questions

Is Memory Match free?

Yes. It is completely free to play in your browser with no account or download needed.

How many pairs are there?

The board has eight pairs, making sixteen cards in total.

How is my best score saved?

Your best move count is stored in your own browser on this device.
