How the reaction test works

  1. Press Start. The box turns red. Wait for it to turn green.
  2. Tap as soon as it turns green. Your time is shown in milliseconds.
  3. Complete five rounds to see your average reaction time.

What is a good reaction time?

Most people react to a visual signal in roughly 200 to 300 milliseconds. Your speed can change with tiredness, distraction and the device you use, so try several times for a fair result.

Tips for a better score

Keep your finger close to the screen before the change, and do not tap early. Tapping too soon restarts the round.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is this reaction test?

It is a fun, approximate test. Your results can vary with the device, browser and your focus, so take several tries for a fair picture.

What is an average reaction time?

Many adults react to visual cues in about 200 to 300 milliseconds, but results vary widely between people.

Does tapping early count?

No. If you tap before the screen turns green, the round restarts so you can try again.
