How to play Snake

  1. Press Start, or any arrow key or swipe to begin.
  2. Steer the snake toward the food to grow longer and earn points.
  3. Avoid the walls and your own tail. The game ends on contact.

Controls on every device

Phone and tablet: swipe on the board or use the on-screen arrows. Laptop and desktop: arrow keys or W, A, S, D.

Why Snake is still fun

The rules are simple, but the game speeds up as you grow and the space narrows. It is a perfect quick game for a short break, and your best score is saved on your device.

Frequently asked questions

Is Snake free to play online?

Yes. You can play this Snake game online for free in your browser without downloading or signing up.

How do I control the snake on my phone?

Swipe in the direction you want to go on the game board, or tap the on-screen arrow buttons below the board.

Does the game save my high score?

Your best score is saved in your own browser on this device. It is not uploaded to a server.
